# BRIEF v2: jamarimyers.com, chaptered index with doors

Interviewed, 2026-10-23. Jamari's words: the v1 split stage was "not close to where I want it";
the new build should be "a little closer to current website", with "more animations", "focus on
content substance", "pillars / paths, deeper dive into my mind and experience", and "content
creation and partnerships" as one of the pillars. He chose: home as a cinematic index with one deep
page per pillar; evolve the current site's look; creator pillar with partners, testimonials and a
dated stats line, no rate card; a "What it taught me" section at the end of each pillar page.
Full plan: /root/.claude/plans/twinkly-tickling-hellman.md (copied below in summary).

The current jamarimyers.com, grown up: deep navy, Instrument Serif with one italic accent word per
heading, atmospheric smoke and glow, hairline grids, uppercase micro labels. Now with real
photography and scroll-driven scenes.

a door each; pillar pages are editorial documents (flow and in, reveal, parallax in media columns,
count in prose). Nav: fixed 56px bar. Hero: atmospheric type on smoke. Close: running-text email.
