# Reference scorecard: jamarimyers.com split stage

## Method and limits

- Mobbin was available. Seven `search_sections` queries ran, five results each, 35 results in all.
- The tool returned low-res inline preview images plus bare metadata (site name, section id, Mobbin URL). It gave no section titles or descriptions. I looked at all 35 previews and describe only what is visible in them. I did not download the high-res images.
- Two previews rendered blank (Aino Agency hero, solid dark; Squarespace hero, solid white). They are not scored.
- Not inspected: wodniack.dev and awwwards.com (blocked by network policy, not attempted). rauno.me, leerob.com and antonandirene.com were not searched because Mobbin worked.
- Fit score is 1 to 5 for the split stage: two hard grounds, no cards, no gradients, powerful but clear.

## Three borrowable ideas for the split-stage build

**1. Seam labels as a ruler (V7, Grok).** Run hairline ticks down the seam, one per act. FIELD and DESK sit either side in small caps. When a side is dragged under about 20 percent width, its label collapses to one letter. At act 4 the active tick turns accent, DESK slides off the seam, and OSMO is the only label left.

**2. Timeline lands in two columns (Faculty Department).** Act 2 is two bare ledgers: field rows on the dark side, desk rows on the light, sharing baselines so a season sits level with a banking summer. Rows reveal as the seam passes and figures count up. Hover a row to underline its twin across the seam. Hairlines and small caps only.

**3. Contact resolves the page (Vucko, MOUTHWASH).** After the collapse the dark column holds one hairline, a small "(Contact)" label, and the address set large and underlined. Nothing else competes. A three-column colophon (Runway's idea, no marquee) sits below on the same ground, and the page ends with the seam parked at the frame edge.

## Refuse list

Seen in the references, and each would make the site generic:

- Blurred pastel gradient grounds with floating offset photos (Oevra), purple 3D blob and "Hey! I'm ..." template header (Framer).
- Circular avatar over a centered paragraph, with carousel arrows and dots (Headspace, Vanta).
- Rounded pastel cards with isometric or illustrated icons, and zig-zag path timelines (Apollo, Going, Surfshark).
- Marquee tickers and all-mono-caps styling (Runway); mega-footers with link grids (The New Yorker, Readymag).
- Slanted-edge photo-plus-tagline splits (TIDAL) and full-bleed tinted face video (Locomotive): both need assets, or recall the filmic grammar that lost.
- Dim-edged gallery strips and carousels (Siena Film Foundation, Rivian, Stripe): there are no photos to carry them.
- Two contact addresses, or any second call to action.
