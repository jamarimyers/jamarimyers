## Voice & themes

- **Builder first.** The GitHub README leads with "Founder of Osmo" and a concrete stack (TypeScript, React Native, Expo, Node.js, Supabase). He presents as someone who ships product, not just ideas.
- **Performance and self-regulation.** Osmo is framed around emotional state and performance in athletes; "human performance" and "applied AI" are named interests. The athlete identity is the lived credential behind the product.
- **Finance as a second discipline.** J.P. Morgan IB analyst, equity research, finance chair role; the site calls him a "finance professional". The pairing of athletics, finance and entrepreneurship is the through-line.
- **Discipline and range, stated through the past tense.** "Former D1 athlete" plus 16 countries, Rome semester, and brand deals signal discipline and cross-cultural fluency; numbers are used as proof points.
- **Community and service.** Chair of Finance at Black in Business, financial literacy workshops for 40+ students, and the pull toward a diverse community in his Brown roster quote.
