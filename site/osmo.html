Building · Osmo

The move after the number.

Osmo gives college athletes a short morning check-in and one next move for the day.

Something I noticed while I was still inside it.

I stopped playing Division I football after my sophomore year at Brown, and the thing that stayed with me was something I had noticed while I was still inside it.

Recovery scores, strain, sleep quality, all of it arrived every morning without context. A low number did not produce a better decision. It produced another thing to worry about before practice had even started.

Every morning I knew my recovery score. Nobody gave me anything for the mornings I woke up flat before a game.

What Osmo is.

Osmo is an emotional performance platform for college athletes, sold to athletic departments.

Physical performance is trained. Emotional performance should be too.

How a morning works.

  1. 01 Check in

    Each athlete starts the day with a short morning check-in.

  2. 02 Context

    An athlete can connect a wearable for context. Osmo has WHOOP developer access and uses WHOOP-derived data from connected users.

  3. 03 One next move

    The athlete leaves with one next move for today.

Osmo works without a wearable.

Built for honest answers.

Athletes choose what is shared; coaches see a simple readiness label, never journals or raw biometrics.

What it taught me

I had been trained to begin with financials and reason backward toward the story. Osmo taught me to begin with the behavior and let the numbers explain it afterward. In fall 2025 that meant 11 interviews with athletes and staff and a 48-person student-athlete survey. I started Osmo to build the missing step, and I begin most problems with the behavior now.
