FieldDesk

Osmo is an emotional performance platform for college athletes, sold to athletic departments.

A short morning check-in, optional wearable context, one next move for today.

Built for honest answers.
Athletes choose what is shared. Coaches see a simple readiness label, never journals or raw biometrics.
Grounded in early research.
Fall 2025, exploratory: 11 interviews and a student-athlete survey (n=48). 90% of those surveyed wanted structured guidance; 43% said what they do now works.
Status.
App built, in release testing with early athletes. Osmo has WHOOP developer access and works without a wearable.

Built with TypeScript, React Native, Expo, Node.js, Supabase.
